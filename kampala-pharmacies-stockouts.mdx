## The Problem Nobody Talks About

Walk into any independently-owned pharmacy in Kampala and ask the owner what keeps them up at night. You'll hear the same things: "We ran out of paracetamol last week." "A supplier delivery didn't come and we lost three days of sales." "We had to throw away 40,000 UGX worth of expired stock."

These aren't isolated incidents. They are the daily reality of running a pharmacy without an intelligent system managing your operations.

## What Manual Stock Management Actually Costs

When a pharmacy runs out of a high-demand item, the immediate cost is visible — lost sales. But the real damage runs deeper:

**Lost customer trust.** A patient who comes in for their monthly refill and finds empty shelves doesn't wait for restock. They walk to the next pharmacy and often don't come back.

**Overstocking the wrong items.** Without demand forecasting, most pharmacies compensate for stockouts by overordering — which locks cash in slow-moving stock and creates expiry risk.

**Supplier relationship gaps.** Late reorders mean emergency phone calls, higher prices, and rushed deliveries that disrupt the entire workflow.

**Staff time drain.** Stock counting, reorder calls, and expiry checks consume hours that should go toward serving customers.
